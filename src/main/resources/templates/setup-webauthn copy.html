🔐 Setup WebAuthn Authentication
ℹ️ What is WebAuthn?

WebAuthn is a secure authentication method that uses your device's biometrics (fingerprint, face) or security keys instead of passwords. Once registered, you'll authenticate automatically in the background without entering credentials.

1 Give Your Device a Name

This helps you identify which device this credential is for (e.g., "My MacBook", "Work iPhone").

2 Register Your Device

Click the button below and follow your browser's prompts. You'll be asked to authenticate using your device's biometric or security method.

3 You're Done!

Once registered, you can authenticate using your device without entering your password again. The authentication happens automatically in the background, similar to SSH keys.
